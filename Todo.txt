

1.  Per-Trade Risk Management

	•	Max floating loss per trade capped at 1.1% of account balance

	•	If floating loss exceeds 1.1% on any position, it is auto-closed immediately

	•	Works even if multiple trades are open on the same instrument



2.  Daily Drawdown Protection

	•	Maximum daily drawdown limit: 2% of account balance

	•	If 2% loss (closed + floating) is hit in a day:

	•	Auto-close all open trades

	•	Block any new trades for the rest of the day
